👉 This dataset represents city-level traffic conditions in the state of Haryana, India.
It contains 2,500 traffic records collected at regular 15‑minute intervals, designed to reflect realistic urban traffic patterns across major cities such as Faridabad, Gurugram, Panipat, Ambala, Hisar, Karnal, Rohtak, Sonipat, Yamunanagar, and Kurukshetra.
Each record includes the date and time of observation, city, vehicle type, junction type, traffic light status, lane count, congestion level, accident reports, and road type.
The dataset is suitable for DBMS implementation, Business Intelligence analysis, traffic congestion studies, data visualization, and predictive modeling.

🌍 Geographic Scope
- State: Haryana
- Cities Covered: Faridabad, Gurugram, Panipat, Ambala, Hisar, Karnal, Rohtak, Sonipat, Yamunanagar, Kurukshetra
- Coverage: 2,500 traffic records across major roads and junctions
- Granularity: Road-level and junction-level traffic observations
- Examples: NH-44 (Panipat), Delhi–Gurugram Expressway, Ambala Cantt Junction, Rohtak Ring Road.
  
📱 Dataset Size
Number of rows: 1000
Number of columns: 15

🎯 Purpose
This dataset is designed for:
- Traffic Flow Analysis – Understanding congestion patterns
- Predictive Modeling – Building ML models for traffic prediction
- Signal Optimization – Analyzing traffic light timings
- Incident Impact Studies – Evaluating accident effects on traffic flow
- Urban Planning – Data-driven decisions for infrastructure improvements
- Research & Education – Academic projects and student learning
